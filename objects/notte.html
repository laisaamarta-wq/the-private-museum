Named for the colour the sky over the Adriatic turns an hour after sunset, when the blue has stopped being day and is not yet night.

Why it is rareAlmost every blue sapphire sold today has been heated to deepen its colour. Eleven matched stones that never were is a rare thing.

Each sapphire was matched twice: once in north daylight, and once again by a single candle. A stone stayed in the collar only if it held its blue in both.

The eleven cushions graduate by less than a tenth of a millimetre from the centre outward. Between them, marquise diamonds work as hinges, so the collar closes around the neck like a hand.
